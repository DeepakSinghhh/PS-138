# Optimization benchmark

Generated by `make bench` (this configuration) — `make bench-quick` is a 3-seed smoke run, `make bench-full` uses 30 seeds and larger budgets. Every algorithm evaluates fleet plans through the same tracker (identical budgets, identical feasibility rules), and front quality is measured on normalised objectives (fuel, WtW emissions, cost) with a common reference front per instance.

Budget: 8,000 plan evaluations per run, 10 seeds per algorithm (large-scale network: 16,000 evaluations, 5 seeds; scalability sweep: 5 seed(s) per size).

Tuning disclosure: QMOEA-H hyperparameters were set on the India instance, with two later exceptions. The rate of the route-wise merge operator (0.5 vs 1.0) was chosen after comparing India, EU and the 50/100-route synthetic networks of the scalability sweep (generator seed 7). Attributing fleet-availability excess to routes (on vs off) was decided on a 3-seed comparison over every network in this report, including the 100-route large-scale network (generator seed 11). No other setting was fitted to the EU or synthetic networks.
